# 07 Accessibility

1. Verify keyboard-only primary flows and visible focus.
2. Apply correct tabs, dialog, combobox, disclosure, table, and live-region semantics.
3. Restore focus when dialogs close and prevent background interaction while open.
4. Pair status color with text/icon; provide chart summaries and map-list parity.
5. Respect reduced motion and keep meaningful text at least 11px.
