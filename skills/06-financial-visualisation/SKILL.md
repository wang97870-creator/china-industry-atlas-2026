# 06 Financial Visualisation

1. Use line charts for trends, bar/dot for numeric comparison, stacked bar for composition, heatmap for gaps, and small multiples for many cities.
2. Radar is secondary only; 3D is forbidden.
3. Prefer ECharts `dataset` and separate data from options.
4. Each chart includes title, unit, period, source, summary, and accessible description.
5. Derived fit scores must be labelled non-investment research indicators.
