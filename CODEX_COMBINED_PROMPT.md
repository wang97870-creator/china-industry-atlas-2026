# V2.1 combined execution brief

The original download referenced by the owner was unavailable in the local filesystem. This repository copy consolidates the two supplied full-text briefs and the owner's final instructions into an auditable execution contract.

Treat the current repository as a deployed production baseline. Use a strangler migration: keep data, maps, licenses, current smoke coverage, and deployability intact while additive modules gradually take over presentation and interaction.

Build a shared-data platform for Explore, Investor, Policy, and Learn. Retain the selected province, city, industry, company, comparison set, and watchlist across mode changes. Provide a command palette, URL state, map/list parity, a city-first research sequence, professional company table, comparison trays, evidence/confidence/completeness, source drawer, and explicit system states.

Explore uses approximately 58% map and 42% research panel on wide screens. Other modes use a full-width task workbench with the map available as a collapsible context panel. The visual system must preserve the deep navy foundation, cyan primary accent, blue-violet secondary accent, and semantic red/amber/green states without decorative glow or unnecessary nested cards.

Do not fabricate missing data. In particular, absent capital projects or policy execution records must be labelled as unavailable or awaiting structured evidence. Derived matching and gap analysis must be labelled as research inference, not company expansion intent or investment advice.

Charts remain ECharts-based and use `dataset` where possible. Trend = line; numeric comparison = bar/dot; composition = stacked bar; gap = matrix/heatmap; multi-city = small multiples; no 3D charts. Every chart needs title, unit, period, source, text summary, and accessible description.

Execute the eight repository skills in order. Do not claim improvement without before/after images, key interaction tests, count comparison, console check, mobile check, keyboard check, visual-difference note, and known trade-offs. Deliver a preview first and do not merge/deploy until the owner approves.
